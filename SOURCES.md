# Audio Asset Sources & Licensing

## Overview

The Café Timelapse soundscape is **100% procedurally synthesised** in real time
using the Web Audio API (`AudioContext`, oscillators, noise buffers, biquad
filters, and gain envelopes). **No external audio files, recordings, or
samples are used.** Because every sound is generated from mathematical
algorithms at runtime, the entire soundtrack is:

- **Royalty-free** — no third-party licensing required.
- **Public-domain** — generated waveforms carry no copyright.
- **Zero-asset** — no files to download, reducing page weight.

## How the audio is generated

All synthesis logic lives in [`public/js/audio-manager.js`](public/js/audio-manager.js).

### Signal graph (four buses)

```
sources ─▶ musicBus    ┐
sources ─▶ ambienceBus ├─▶ masterBus (gain) ─▶ destination
sources ─▶ machineBus  ┘
```

Each bus is an independent `GainNode`, allowing per-layer volume control and
cross-fade.

### Music layer

Each era has a procedurally-composed musical groove generated by a
`MusicEngine`:

| Era  | Music source    | Style                  | Scale            | Tempo (BPM) | Chord progression |
|------|-----------------|------------------------|------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1945 | Wireless radio  | Swing-era big-band     | Major pentatonic | 128         | Eb – Cm – Fm – Bb |
| 1965 | Jukebox         | Motown soul            | Minor pentatonic | 112         | Am – F – C – G    |
| 1985 | Boombox         | Synth-pop / new wave   | Natural minor    | 124         | Dm – Bb – C – Dm  |
| 2005 | iPod dock       | Indie chillout         | Major            | 96          | G – D – Em – C    |
| 2025 | Phone/speaker   | Lo-fi chill beats      | Dorian           | 74          | Fm – Bb           |

The engine cycles through a chord progression, playing:

- **Chord pad** — 3–4 detuned oscillators (sawtooth/triangle/square) through a
  low-pass filter.
- **Bass line** — root/fifth alternation with a dedicated bass voice.
- **Melody** — scale-degree walker constrained to the era's scale.
- **Drums** — kick (sine drop), snare (filtered noise), hi-hat (high-passed
  noise), with era-specific patterns (brushes, soul, electronic, soft, lo-fi).

### Ambience layer

Each era has a `AmbienceEngine` providing the café background sound:

- **Conversation murmur** — brown/pink noise through a band-pass filter,
  modulated by a slow LFO (0.18 Hz) to simulate the ebb-and-flow of chatter.
- **Cup clatter** — short, high-resonance filtered-noise bursts at random
  intervals (1.5–5.5 s), simulating ceramic cup clinks.
- **Era-specific textures:**
  - 1965: Jukebox mechanical hum (60 Hz sine).
  - 1985: Cassette tape hiss (high-passed white noise).
  - 2025: Vinyl crackle (band-passed pink noise + random pops).

### Machine SFX layer

Each era has a `MachineEngine` providing coffee-machine sounds:

- **Idle hum** — continuous low-frequency oscillator (50–90 Hz).
- **Espresso shot** — filtered pink-noise hiss burst (1.2–1.5 s).
- **Grinder** — gritty band-passed brown-noise burst (0.8–1.4 s).
- **Steam wand** — high-passed white-noise burst (2–3.5 s).

Machine types differ by era:

| Era  | Machine type         | Characteristics                                    |
|------|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| 1945 | Lever espresso       | Manual lever, quieter idle, longer shots           |
| 1965 | Lever espresso       | Manual lever, medium character                     |
| 1985 | Pump espresso        | Electric pump hum, medium shots                    |
| 2005 | Automatic espresso   | Automatic, pump hum, shorter shots, higher pitch   |
| 2025 | Automatic espresso   | Fully automatic, quietest, highest pitch           |

### Noise generation

Three noise colours are synthesised algorithmically:

- **White noise** — uniform random samples (`Math.random() * 2 - 1`).
- **Pink noise** — Paul Kellet's refined filter algorithm (−3 dB/oct).
- **Brown noise** — integrated white noise (−6 dB/oct).

## Cross-fade behaviour

On era change, the `AudioManager.crossFadeTo()` method:

1. Fades the outgoing era's music + ambience from full → silence over **1.5 s**
   (ease via linear ramp on `GainNode`).
2. Fades the incoming era's music + ambience from silence → full over **1.5 s**.
3. Swaps the machine SFX on the transition tick (outgoing fades out over 0.45 s,
   incoming fades in over 0.75 s starting at 30% of the cross-fade).

## Autoplay policy compliance

Audio does **not** autoplay on page load. The `AudioContext` is created in a
`suspended` state and only `resume()`d after the first user gesture
(`pointerdown`, `keydown`, or `touchstart`), in compliance with browser
autoplay policies.

## Volume & mute persistence

The master volume (0–1) and mute state are persisted to `localStorage` under
the keys `cafe-audio-volume` and `cafe-audio-muted` respectively, so user
preferences survive page reloads and era changes.
